Chain them: jab → strong → launcher launches the foe, then jump + H to juggle for air extensions. Press M to watch the two JEV brains spar.
The triangle: throw beats block, attack beats throw, block beats attack — so T punishes a foe who holds Space. Blocking only chips, so defense is never free.
Guard has a height, and each stance has exactly one wrong answer: a standing block eats highs and mids but not I sweep (low); a crouching block eats lows and ground strikes but not H air-kick (overhead); T throw beats both and is unblockable, but only catches a grounded foe — jump it. A blocked strike leaves a few frames where you can cancel straight into the low or the grab: that is the tick throw, and it is why holding guard is a decision, not a habit. Meter builds from hits you land: spend 50 on B to burst out of a combo, or all 100 on G for the super, which cancels out of any confirmed hit.
