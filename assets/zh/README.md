The English documents are not literal translations. They keep every number but reframe the argument
for a reader who did not write the original — in particular the capability record, whose English
version leads with the boundary findings rather than with the chronology of how they were reached.

Why keep both: the Chinese record is the primary evidence, including the parts that are unflattering
to the conclusions drawn at the time. In `JEV_CAPABILITY_RECORD.md` §2.2 and §4, claims are recorded
that were later falsified, with the measurement that falsified them, so a reader can check the
reasoning rather than only its conclusion. That audit trail does not survive translation, so the
original stays.
